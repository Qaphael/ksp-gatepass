# KSP GatePass staff guide

## Before sales open

1. An administrator signs in and confirms the correct event, date, venue, capacity, ticket categories, prices and admission policy.
2. Confirm seller, verifier and supervisor accounts are active and have the intended roles. Never share a login.
3. Generate tickets and print one sample. Check its serial and QR code before printing the production batch.
4. Sellers record each confirmed sale and each amount actually received. A payment entry is a staff record; it is not a bank or mobile-money provider confirmation.

## At the entrance

1. Open **Scan QR** or **Manual check-in**, select the event and enter the gate label configured for that entrance.
2. For QR admission, allow camera access and hold the printed QR in frame. For manual lookup, enter the printed serial, review the result and press **Confirm admission**.
3. Admit a guest only after the server displays the green **VALID — ADMITTED** result.
4. A duplicate, unpaid, cancelled, revoked, expired or unknown ticket is not admitted. Ask the guest to wait and refer the case to an authorised supervisor; do not bypass the server result.
5. If **OFFLINE — CHECK-IN UNAVAILABLE** appears, pause admission. Do not treat a camera read, a previous screen or a phone's local state as permission to admit.
6. Use **Attendance** to check server-recorded admissions. Administrators can add a reasoned incident note to a check-in; the original admission record remains in the history.

## Reconciliation and reports

Use **Reports** to select an event and report, then apply an optional date range. Available reports depend on the account role. CSV is for analysis; PDF is for reading or filing. Reconciliation separates gross sales, discounts, net sales, staff-recorded payments, reversals, refunds and current balances. It also shows that actual cash counts are not recorded in this release. No report describes staff-entered payments as independently verified receipts.

Administrators can use **Audit log** to search critical actions by event, action, target or outcome. Audit summaries must not be copied into public channels because they may contain operational details.

## Event close

1. Close sales and finish admission through the event controls.
2. Review ticket inventory, sales, payment totals, cancellations/refunds, outstanding balances, print history, attendance and scan attempts.
3. Export the reports needed by the organiser and compare cash with the physical count outside GatePass.
4. Keep printed ticket stock and exported reports in the organiser's approved secure location.

## Escalation

Contact the event administrator for incorrect event settings, account access, disputed payments, suspected duplicate tickets, or a network outage. Only an administrator may record a check-in correction, and a reason plus the exact ticket serial are required.
