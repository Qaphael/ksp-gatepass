# Seller guide

## Before selling

Sign in with your own account, choose the correct event, and confirm that the tickets you will hand out have been issued to you. Keep issued paper stock secure. Agree with the organiser how cash will be counted and handed over; GatePass records staff reports and does not count or verify physical cash.

## Scan and confirm a sale

1. Open **Scan to sell**, select the event, and scan the exact ticket you are handing to the buyer. Check that the screen shows **Ready to sell** and the correct category and price.
2. Enter the buyer's name and phone when requested. Check the amount received and payment method. With full payment required, the entire total must be recorded before a seller sale can be confirmed.
3. Confirm the sale only after the buyer has paid and you are handing over that ticket. Give the buyer the ticket and receipt according to event procedure.
4. If the connection fails or the result is unclear, wait for the server response and retry the same sale. Do not hand out a ticket based on a camera read or an unconfirmed screen.

## What the scan messages mean

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Ready to sell** | Check category and price, then continue with buyer and payment details. |
| **Already sold** | Do not sell this ticket. Keep it and ask the supervisor to investigate. |
| **Held by another seller** / **Not issued to you** | Do not sell it. Return it to the stock keeper for reconciliation. |
| **Different event** | Check the selected event; do not sell it for this event. |
| **Invalid QR** / **Unknown serial** | Try the scan steps below. If it still fails, stop and ask the stock keeper or supervisor. |
| **Cancelled** / **Revoked** | Do not sell or hand over the ticket. Give it to the supervisor. |
| **Sales closed** / **Event not selling** / **Category inactive** | Stop sales and contact the event administrator. |
| **Connection failed** | Sales cannot be confirmed until the server responds. Keep the ticket and retry when connected. |

## If a QR will not scan

Wipe the camera lens, use bright even light, hold the phone steady, and fit the whole QR inside the frame. Avoid glare, folds and shadows. If it still fails, check that you selected the right event and use the printed serial lookup if your account and event policy allow it. Never guess a serial or use another ticket's QR. Escalate repeated failures; do not sell an unconfirmed ticket.

## Cash handover

Keep sale records and cash together by payment method. At the agreed handover, count cash with the receiver, compare the physical count with the staff-recorded totals, and have the authorised receiver record the remittance. The remittance is a record of what staff say was handed over, not independent payment verification. Keep the signed issue/return paperwork and report any difference to the supervisor before close-out.
