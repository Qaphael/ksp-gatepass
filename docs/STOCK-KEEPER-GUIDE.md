# Stock keeper guide

## Issue tickets

1. Confirm the event and seller account before handing out stock.
2. In **Stock**, issue the selected serials to that seller. Check the seller, quantity and serial range on screen.
3. Print or save the issue slip, count the physical tickets together, and have both people acknowledge the handover. Keep the slip for reconciliation.
4. Store unissued stock securely. Never issue a serial already held by another seller.

## Returns and losses

- **Return:** count and scan/select unsold tickets, check the displayed serials, record the return, and take physical custody of the tickets. Returned tickets go back to the unissued pool.
- **Loss or damage:** verify the serial with the seller, record the specific reason, and confirm the loss. A lost ticket is revoked and cannot be sold or admitted. Keep damaged paper separate from live stock.
- Do not return a sold ticket as available. Ask an administrator to handle a sale correction through the approved linked correction workflow.

## Seller close-out

For each seller, reconcile issued stock into sold, returned and lost tickets. Review the outstanding serial list and resolve every ticket before closing the seller. GatePass blocks close-out while any issued ticket is unaccounted for. Compare system totals with physical stock and the seller's signed slips; record discrepancies and escalate them instead of guessing.

## Remittances

1. Count money with the seller and receiver present; agree the amount and method.
2. Record the event, seller, amount in whole UGX, payment method and receiving staff member. Add a short note if useful.
3. Keep the signed handover record with the event paperwork. GatePass records the staff-reported handover and does not verify that cash or a transfer exists.
4. Never edit or delete a remittance to correct it. Ask an authorised administrator to record a linked reversal with a reason, then enter the corrected handover as a new remittance.

Keep issue slips, returns, loss notes and remittance evidence together until the administrator completes event reconciliation.
